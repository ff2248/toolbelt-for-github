# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Toolbelt for GitHub doesn't collect, store, or transmit any data.

- It runs only on pages under `https://github.com/`.
- It looks at the page only to find GitHub's comment forms and add buttons next to them. It doesn't read comment content.
- When you click a button, it submits GitHub's own form through your existing browser session, just as if you'd used GitHub's menu yourself.
- It makes no network requests of its own, uses no third-party services, and stores nothing in your browser.

Any change to this policy will be published in this file.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ff2248/toolbelt-for-github/issues).
